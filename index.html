What can you do with Elite Elliott?

Call Your Shots

Post chart calls with real conviction

Share a ticker, a sentiment, and a timeframe — bullish, bearish or neutral — and put your read on the tape in front of the network.

Every call is timestamped and tracked, so your win rate speaks for itself instead of a highlight reel of your best trades.

Build Your Circle

Follow traders you actually rate

Find setups worth watching, follow the people posting them, and build a feed that reflects the strategies and tickers you care about.

Mutual follows unlock direct messages, so the best conversations don't stay stuck in public replies.

Stay In The Loop

Real-time chat, trending tickers & news

Message your network the moment a setup develops, track the tickers heating up across the feed, and pull in Nepal and world market news without leaving the app.

One place for the calls, the conversation and the context — no five-app juggling act.
